# StudyPath Quick Start Guide

Get your semester-based planner set up in 5 minutes!

## Step 1: Create Your First Semester (1 minute)

1. Click **"Subjects"** in the navigation
2. Click **"+ New Semester"** button
3. Enter:
   - **Name**: `1/2025` (or your semester)
   - **Start Date**: `2025-08-01` (first day of semester)
   - **End Date**: `2025-12-31` (last day of semester)
4. Click **"Create"**

✅ Your first semester is now active!

## Step 2: Add Your First Subject (2 minutes)

1. Click **"+ Add Subject"** button
2. In the **Basic Info tab**:
   - **Code**: `POL2200` (from your course list)
   - **Title**: `Thai Politics`
   - **Credits**: `3`
   - **Lecturer**: `Dr. Smith` (optional)
   - **Difficulty**: `Medium`
3. Click **"Save Subject"**

✅ Your first subject is created!

## Step 3: Add Class Schedule (1 minute)

1. Click the subject card → **Edit** button
2. Go to **Schedule tab**
3. Click **"+ Add"**
4. Set:
   - **Day**: `Monday`
   - **Start Time**: `09:00`
   - **End Time**: `10:30`
   - **Room**: `101` (optional)
5. Click **"Save Subject"**

✅ Your class is now on the calendar!

## Step 4: Add Exams (1 minute)

1. Click the subject card → **Edit** button
2. Go to **Exams tab**
3. Click **"+ Add"**
4. Set:
   - **Type**: `Final`
   - **Date**: `2025-08-15`
   - **Start**: `09:00`
   - **End**: `11:00`
5. Click **"Save Subject"**

✅ Your exam is scheduled!

## Step 5: Track Progress

### On the Dashboard:
- **Daily Focus**: Shows your top priority subject
- **Mark Video Watched**: Click to increment progress
- **Monitor GPA**: See your semester GPA update

### In Calendar:
- **Month View**: See all events at a glance
- **Week View**: Detailed class schedule
- **Upcoming Events**: Next 7 days preview

## Daily Usage

### Morning Routine
1. Open **Dashboard**
2. Check **Daily Focus** subject
3. Click **"Mark video watched"** as you study
4. Review **Focus Subjects** list below

### Check Schedule
1. Open **Calendar**
2. Switch between Month/Week view
3. Click **"Today"** button to jump to today

### Update Progress
1. Open **Subjects**
2. Click **Edit** on any subject
3. Go to **Progress tab**
4. Update:
   - **Status**: What stage you're in
   - **Progress slider**: 0-100%
   - **Videos watched**: Track completed lectures

### Prepare for Exams
1. Open **Calendar** → **Upcoming Events**
2. See exam countdown
3. Increase study status to "revision"
4. Adjust progress percentage

## Troubleshooting

**Q: Subjects not showing?**
- Check that semester is active (highlighted tab)
- Refresh the page if needed

**Q: Exams not in calendar?**
- Verify dates are formatted correctly (YYYY-MM-DD)
- Check exam is in active semester

**Q: GPA not updating?**
- Set subject status to "completed"
- Ensure credits are assigned (1-4)

**Q: Can't edit subject?**
- Click on the subject card
- Look for Edit button (hover to reveal)
- Make sure you're in the right semester

**Q: Lost data?**
- Check login status (top of page)
- Data syncs with Firebase when logged in
- Local storage backup always available
